Method and limits

  • Raw inputs: 2021-25 nflverse and cfbfastR play-by-play. Efficiency states use EPA/play, success rate, pace and opponent-relative updates.
  • Walk-forward: ratings update after each game. Training uses 2021-23; the held-out test is 2024-25 against recorded closing spreads.
  • Roster prior: every offseason, NFL team identity is regressed 42% and CFB identity 58% toward average. That is a conservative turnover proxy, not player-level tracking.
  • Bet rules: fixed before testing: 3+ points NFL, 4+ CFB. ROI assumes -110 and excludes pushes.
  • Limits: no player-level injury data, transfers, coaching quality, weather or live price shopping in the historical model. CFB score extraction and ESPN closing lines should be audited before using real money. NFL v2 still fails. A complete point-in-time national CFB injury and transfer archive was unavailable, so CFB v2 was not fabricated or backtested with leaked data.

How this ranking works

This season only. Every 2026 FBS final through Nov 8 (685 FBS-involving games) feeds the ranking. No preseason talent composite, returning-production, portal, draft-loss or AP-poll inputs are used, and no prior-season results carry in.

Results first. Wins and losses drive 75% of the rating through an opponent-adjusted (Colley) win-loss system: beating a strong team counts more than beating a weak one, losing to a weak team costs more than losing to a strong one, and strength of schedule comes out of the results themselves rather than being assumed. Scoring and yardage performance (each opponent-adjusted, equally weighted) supplies the remaining 25%, weighted so underlying stats support and separate similar resumes rather than drive the ranking.

Head-to-head. When two teams sit within 1.5 rating points, this season's head-to-head winner ranks ahead, applied transitively through chains of opponents. Circular cases (A beat B, B beat C, C beat A) fall back to the full-season resume instead of forcing a contradiction. Affected rows note the deciding result.

Scale and context. The number is an unvalidated resume rating scale, not a calibrated expected margin. Records and the AP poll (Nov 8) are context, not inputs; arrows show rank difference from AP, not movement over time. Injuries are not modeled.
