Method and limits

  • Raw inputs: 2021-25 nflverse and cfbfastR play-by-play. Efficiency states use EPA/play, success rate, pace and opponent-relative updates.
  • Walk-forward: ratings update after each game. Training uses 2021-23; the held-out test is 2024-25 against recorded closing spreads.
  • Roster prior: every offseason, NFL team identity is regressed 42% and CFB identity 58% toward average. That is a conservative turnover proxy, not player-level tracking.
  • Bet rules: fixed before testing: 3+ points NFL, 4+ CFB. ROI assumes -110 and excludes pushes.
  • Limits: no player-level injury data, transfers, coaching quality, weather or live price shopping in the historical model. CFB score extraction and ESPN closing lines should be audited before using real money. NFL v2 still fails. A complete point-in-time national CFB injury and transfer archive was unavailable, so CFB v2 was not fabricated or backtested with leaked data.

How these ratings work

Last season still matters. Points scored/allowed and total yards gained/allowed blend the latest four 2026 games with 2025 full-season averages. Current-season weight is games played ÷ (games played + 2): after 4 games, 67% current / 33% prior; after 5, 71% / 29%; after 6, 75% / 25%. These are feature weights, not shares of the final rating.

Passing/rushing splits, turnovers and QB stats use recent-four averages, with prior-year fallbacks for missing data. Roster changes, coaching, talent and AP points also feed the model. Coefficients were fitted on 3,253 eligible games from 2022-2025. The current snapshot includes completed results through Oct 4.

No explicit opponent-strength or head-to-head adjustment. Records are shown for context, not used directly as a win-loss feature. An earlier win does not force a team above the opponent it beat. Injuries are not modeled.

Subtract two ratings for the model margin before its fitted home adjustment. The AP-removed view subtracts only AP's contribution from the same fitted model. It keeps last-season priors and is a sensitivity check, not a separately trained or validated model.

Measurement only - these rankings are not picks or a validated betting edge.
