Cougars overcome 10-point third-quarter deficit; Running back Makhi Hughes rushes for 158 yards and three TDs in freshman quarterback Keisean Henderson’s first start

MORGANTOWN, W.Va. (AP) – ![Recap Graphic vs West Virginia](https://uhcougars.com/images/2026/11/21/Screenshot_2026-11-21_at_4.14.58_PM.png) University of Houston senior running back Makhi Hughes rushed for a season-high 158 yards and three touchdowns, and junior kicker Jonathan Dimas booted a 23-yard field goal as time expired to lift the #20/21/20 University of Houston Football program to a 34-31 win over West Virginia inside Milan Puskar Stadium on Saturday afternoon. Houston trailed 31-21 late in the third quarter before scoring 13 points in the final 15:45 to claim the win in its first trip to Morgantown. Making the first start of his collegiate career, freshman quarterback Keisean Henderson threw for 268 yards and a touchdown and teamed with Hughes to lead the Cougars on the final game-winning drive. After the Houston defense forced West Virginia into a 3-and-out from its 10-yard line with the score tied at 31 and only 94 seconds left, Henderson guided the Cougars to the Mountaineer 4-yard line. He rushed for four yards and threw two completions for 18 yards, including a 6-yard pass to senior tight end Patrick Overmyer on third down to extend the final drive. Hughes rushed three times for 12 yards on the final drive to set the stage for Dimas' heroics. With the win, Houston improved to 8-3 overall and 5-3 in Big 12 play. West Virginia fell to 5-6 and 2-5 in Big 12 action. Henderson, who was the nation's No. 1 quarterback prospect out of high school, and Hughes helped the Cougars rack up 132 yards of offense and convert 5-of-6 third downs at one point in the fourth quarter alone. Fifth-year wide receiver Trent Walker set up the Cougar offense on the final drive, returning a punt 22 yards to the West Virginia 38. Filling in after senior starter Conner Weigman was sidelined with an upper body injury, Henderson gave the Cougars the lead on their third play from scrimmage with a 68-yard touchdown pass to junior wide receiver Amare Thomas. He had runs of eight and nine yards to set up Hughes' 3-yard TD run, which tied the score at 31-31 with 5:25 remaining. West Virginia's Cam Cook ran for 131 yards, including an 8-yard TD run.
