# Keisean Henderson leads Houston to win after QB Conner Weigman ruled out

- ESPN News Services

Nov 21, 2026, 12:22 PM ET

MORGANTOWN, W.Va. -- True freshman Keisean Henderson, the No. 4 overall recruit in the 2026 ESPN 300, threw a scoring pass in his first start in place of the injured Conner Weigman on Saturday as No. 20 Houston beat West Virginia 34-31 on a field goal as time expired.

Makhi Hughes rushed for a season-high 158 yards and three touchdowns for the Cougars (8-3, 5-3 Big 12), who got the win on a 23-yard field goal by Jonathan Dimas.

Weigman was listed as a game-time decision and expected to start for the Cougars, but he was in street clothes while watching his teammates warm up and was ruled out just before kickoff with an undisclosed injury.

The fifth-year senior has thrown for 2,552 yards with 25 touchdowns and just three interceptions. He has also rushed for 308 yards and another seven scores.

West Virginia (5-6, 2-6) couldn't hold on to a 10-point lead and has lost three straight for the second time this season. Now the Mountaineers must win their regular-season finale to become bowl eligible.

Henderson did just enough against the Power 4's worst defense to give the Cougars a chance in the end. He finished 18-of-28 for 268 yards. He had runs of 8 and 9 yards to set up Hughes' 3-yard TD run that tied the score at 31 with 5:25 remaining.

Henderson was ESPN's top dual-threat quarterback in the 2026 recruiting class after throwing for 6,415 yards and 76 touchdowns while rushing for 661 yards and 14 scores in four high school seasons.

After finding out in pregame warmups that he'd be making his first career start, Houston freshman Keisean Henderson finished 18 of 28 for 268 yards and a touchdown as he led No. 20 Houston to a 34-31 victory against West Virginia. Frank Jansky/Icon Sportswire

West Virginia quarterback Michael Hawkins Jr. was shaken up on a hard tackle late in the fourth quarter and did not return.

On its final five drives, West Virginia missed a 42-yard field goal attempt and punted four times. Trent Walker returned the Mountaineers' final punt 22 yards to the West Virginia 38, and then Houston took the ball inside the 5 to set up the winning kick.

West Virginia's Cam Cook ran for 131 yards, including an 8-yard TD run, and the Mountaineers had 329 yards on the ground -- the fourth time this season they have surpassed 300 yards.

The Associated Press contributed to this report.

Henderson, Yates Receive Weekly Big 12 Conference Honors - University of Houston Athletics
11/23/2026 12:00:00 PM | Football
Henderson shined in his first start while Yates posted career-high tackles
**HOUSTON –**** **Houston Football quarterback Keisean Henderson was named the Big 12 Conference Freshman of the Week and linebacker Jaden Yates earned the league's Defensive Player of the Week on Monday.
Henderson stepped up in his first career start, throwing for 268 yards and a touchdown. Staying calm under pressure, he orchestrated a 10-point comeback in the second half, including an 87-yard game-tying drive late in the fourth quarter.
The Houston native was the first Houston true freshman quarterback to win his first career start since Logan Holgorsen in 2019. He's also the third FBS freshman signal-caller to throw for 250+ yards without an interception on the road this season. He helped the Houston offense post 490 yards of total offense, a program record in a Big 12 game, while also breaking the program record for longest pass in a Big 12 game with a 68-yard dart to Amare Thomas for a touchdown on the team's opening drive.
Yates posted career-highs in both tackles and tackles-for-loss with 16 and 2.5, respectively. His 16 stops were the most by a Cougar since Austin Robinson posted 21 on Oct. 20, 2018 against Navy. The linebacker saw action on 83 snaps, the third most of any player at his position in the country in week 12, and his 76.6 PFF Defense grade was the eighth highest of any FBS linebacker and highest of a Big 12 linebacker with at least 70 plays.
