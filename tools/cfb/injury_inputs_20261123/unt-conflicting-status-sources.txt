# North Texas QB Tayven Jackson out with upper-body injury

- ESPN

Nov 17, 2026, 03:38 PM ET

North Texas senior quarterback Tayven Jackson, who is sixth in the FBS with 3,005 passing yards, will miss at least the next two games with an upper-body injury, coach Neal Brown said Tuesday.

Jackson has 23 touchdown passes and six interceptions, and he has also rushed for four TDs this season. He was injured on the first drive of the second half in Saturday's loss to UTSA.

Chris Jimerson, who has seen limited action this season, is in line to start Saturday's game at Tulane, as Chaston Ditta also has been ruled out with an undisclosed injury.

The Mean Green (7-3) are tied with UTSA and Memphis atop the American Conference at 5-1 in league play.

Brown is hoping that Jackson can return for the American Conference title game, if UNT makes it, as well as a bowl game.

"I'm not ready to say he's out for the rest of the season," Brown said.

Jackson has also played at UCF, Indiana and Tennessee in his college career, starting 25 games while throwing for 6,456 yards and 39 touchdowns with 20 interceptions.

# Chris Jimerson News: Accounts for TD in first start
Written by RotoWire Staff
Published on November 23, 2026
Jimerson was 8-for-16 passing for 124 yards and one interception during Saturday's 28-24 defeat to Tulane. He also had six rushes for 20 yards with one touchdown.
Jimerson accounted for a touchdown in his first start as North Texas' QB1, rushing for a six-yard score in the first quarter. He took over under center after Tayven Jackson suffered an injury in Week 11 against UTSA, and he found the end zone on the ground for the second consecutive game. The quarterback was inefficient through the air, completing exactly half of his attempts, and he was intercepted once. Despite his underwhelming performance, Jimerson should remain the starter under center heading into the team's regular-season finale against UAB on Saturday.
