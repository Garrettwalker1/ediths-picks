Colts release unofficial depth chart for Week 11
The Colts released their depth chart for Thursday night's game against the Houston Texans on Monday.
Nov 16, 2026 at 04:17 PM
Abby Halpin
Contributing Writer
Copied!
The biggest change in the depth chart this week is linebacker CJ Allen moving up to MLB1. Akeem Davis-Gaither was placed on injured reserve Saturday with a knee injury, sidelining him until, at the earliest, Week 15 against the Tennessee Titans. Linebacker West Weeks was signed to the active roster from the practice squad yesterday, and he currently sits at MLB3 below.
Tight end Mo Alie-Cox (groin) was also placed on IR Saturday, moving Drew Ogletree up one spot in the depth chart, followed by Will Mallory and Pharaoh Brown.
Cornerback Johnathan Edwards was signed to the 53-man roster from the practice squad Monday afternoon. Sauce Gardner and Charvarius Ward Sr. both left Sunday's win over the Miami Dolphins with injuries.
